Praxly Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Praxly is a free educational programming tool maintained by Dr. Chris Mayfield in the Department of Computer Science at James Madison University. This policy explains what Praxly collects, why, and who else sees it.

Using Praxly without signing in

The code editor, the translator, and the interpreter all run inside your browser. If you do not sign in and do not use the AI Assistant, the code you write is never sent to us.

Three things still happen in that case:

  • Your work is saved on your own device rather than on our servers, so it survives a page reload. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • We count page views with GoatCounter, which records the page visited, referrer, and a rough browser/country guess. It sets no cookies and does not track you across sites.
  • Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address as part of serving those files.

Signing in

There are two ways in. You can use your own Google account, or a Praxly account that we create for you. Praxly accounts are issued manually, to a class or a collaborator, and are not something you can register for yourself.

Either way we store the same three things: an account identifier, your email address, and your name. For Google sign-in we ask only for your basic profile (openid email profile); we do not store your profile picture, and we never receive your Google password.

The AI Assistant

The AI Assistant is the only feature that sends your work off your device. When you use it, we store your conversation — every message you send and every response you receive — in our database, linked to your account, so the assistant has context and so you can return to past chats.

To answer your prompts, we send the following to a language model provider:

  • your message and the earlier messages in that conversation,
  • the code currently open in your editor,
  • instructions that tell the model how to tutor, and your student/teacher setting.

Where that goes depends on the model in use. Praxly accounts default to a model we host ourselves, running on Ollama behind our own proxy — those conversations reach no outside AI company at all. Otherwise the model runs at Google, Anthropic, or OpenAI, reached through the same proxy. We do not send your name, email, or account identifier to any outside provider, but their own terms govern what they do with the content they receive. Note in particular that Google's free Gemini tier uses submitted prompts to improve Google's products; paid tiers and the other providers do not train on this content.

We also record each AI request to Langfuse, a monitoring tool we run on our own server, to debug problems and improve the tutoring prompts. Those records include the full request and response along with your account identifier, and they are more detailed than what the app shows you.

Please do not put sensitive personal information into the AI Assistant. The app asks you to agree to this before your first chat.

If you supply your own API key

You may connect your own API key for Google, Anthropic, or OpenAI. Praxly accounts do not need one, since they default to the model we host. If you signed in with Google, supplying a key is the only way to use the AI Assistant.

Your key is kept in your browser's local storage and attached to each AI request, so our server can forward it to the provider on your behalf. We never write it to our database or logs. Because local storage is unencrypted, do not use a personal API key on a shared or public computer.

What is stored in your browser

  • your current program, editor layout, and text size,
  • your sign-in token and its expiry,
  • your list of past chats (titles and dates, not message text),
  • your tutor preferences and whether you accepted the AI terms,
  • your own API key, if you supplied one.

Signing out clears your token, your chat list, and your API key. Signing in to a Praxly account also sets short-lived kc-callback-* cookies during the login round trip.

Sharing links

Share and embed links encode your program directly into the URL. Anyone with the link can read that program, and it may be logged by any server or service the link passes through.

Who can see your data

Your chats are visible to you and to the maintainers of Praxly. We do not sell your data, show advertising, or share your information with anyone beyond the providers described above, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

How long something stays depends on what it is.

  • Monitoring records. The Langfuse records described above are deleted after 90 days. They exist to help us fix problems, and old ones are no use for that.
  • Chats and your account. Your conversations stay for as long as you are using Praxly. If you do not sign in for two years, we delete your account and everything attached to it, including every chat and message.

You do not have to wait for either clock. You can delete chats yourself at any time, or ask us to remove your account sooner — see Your choices, below.

If we use Praxly data in a research study, we export what the study needs and keep that copy under the study's own data management plan. The schedule above governs what stays inside Praxly; it does not reach a copy that has already left it.

Your choices

  • You can use Praxly without signing in, and without the AI Assistant.
  • You can delete any chat, or individual messages, from within the app.
  • To delete your account and everything associated with it, including the monitoring records that in-app deletion does not reach, email mayfiecs@jmu.edu and we will do it manually. You can request a copy of your data the same way.

Children

Praxly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic to Praxly and its backend is encrypted with HTTPS, and access to the database is limited to the maintainers. Praxly is a small research and teaching project, not a hardened commercial service, and we cannot guarantee that any system is completely secure.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Praxly after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy, deletion requests, and data requests all go to mayfiecs@jmu.edu.
